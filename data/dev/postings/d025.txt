Your team

The Chief Risk Officer (CRO) division of Julius Baer is recruiting for its 18-month Flex-Track Graduate Programme in the Analytics & Transformation team in Zurich. The team works on risk analytics, predictive modelling and data-driven risk monitoring. During the programme you complete three rotations across teams around the world, including an international assignment.

YOUR CHALLENGE

What to work on?
- Design, build and maintain statistically sound solutions to the problems of our internal clients
- Work with business analysts and CRO teams on risk questions using scientific methods
- Analyse complex data sets and show trends to risk, compliance and senior management
- Propose new opportunities for the analytics roadmap

What to learn?
- Building predictive and prescriptive models for the analytics roadmap
- Implementing analytics solutions for banking processes with colleagues worldwide
- Turning data and methods into risk monitoring strategies
- Explaining the value of data and analytics solutions

YOUR PROFILE
- A Master's degree from a university or university of applied sciences with above-average grades in a quantitative field such as financial engineering, mathematics or physics
- Graduation date no more than 12 months ago
- Initial practical experience analysing data sets and building models in R or Python
- Experience navigating SQL databases
- Enthusiasm for applying data and statistics to complex problems
- A curious researcher who questions ideas and is open to other views
- A team player with a global mindset, strong interpersonal skills and an appetite for learning
- Fluency in English; additional languages are a plus
- Eligibility to work in the country where you apply

What you get

An individual career path, fast learning, real impact and regular exchange with experienced colleagues. You can help shape your second and third rotation.
