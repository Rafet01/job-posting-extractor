Research Internship – Data Analyst 80 - 100 %
Wyss Zurich Translational Center
Zürich

Wyss Zurich is a joint accelerator of ETH Zurich and the University of Zurich, made possible by a donation from a Swiss philanthropist. It brings together multidisciplinary teams to move scientific discoveries towards application. One of its projects, Recolony, works on cancer immunology. We are looking for a research intern to analyse its data.

Start of employment: 2nd of May or upon agreement, temporary.

Your responsibilities
- Review the literature and analyse quantitative research data
- Curate, clean and integrate biological and clinical data sets
- Work with OMICS data from NGS, metabolomics and proteomics
- Implement, test and optimise methods such as variational autoencoders
- Run neural network and statistical analyses in PyTorch, Python and R
- Write a scientific report and document your findings
- Collaborate with an interdisciplinary research team

Your profile
- You are currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's programme in bioinformatics, computational biology, data science, biostatistics or a related field
- A strong background in biological or clinical data analysis and quantitative research
- You are proficient in Python and have data science experience with PyTorch as well as machine learning experience
- You are comfortable with OMICS data (NGS, metabolomics, proteomics)
- You work independently and ask for guidance when you need it
- Strong scientific communication skills and the ability to write structured reports
- Knowledge of command-line tools and Linux-based workflows is a plus

Information on your application

Flexible working models, continuing education, a joint language centre with ETH, sports, discounts and support for international staff.
