Climate Data Science Intern
MSCI
Zurich

Your Team Responsibilities

The MSCI Climate Risk Center brings together climate researchers, financial modellers and software engineers. We turn environmental challenges into financial metrics that help large investors make decisions. This 6-month internship lets you contribute to that work. The role can be based in either our Zurich or our Geneva office.

Your Key Responsibilities
- Develop methods, models, metrics and interactive tools that assess climate risk in investment portfolios
- Contribute to software and data science projects on climate risk, including data analysis, statistical modelling and software development
- Work in a cross-functional team of researchers, analysts, technologists and client-facing colleagues
- Anticipate challenges and adapt quickly to change
- Write, maintain and validate model documentation

Your skills and experience that will help you excel
- Experience in data manipulation and software development, particularly with Python, Pandas and NumPy
- A background in machine learning and artificial intelligence
- A demonstrated interest in climate science, environmental modelling and sustainability
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- You are self-motivated, proactive and think logically

What we offer you
- Transparent compensation and benefits tailored to your location
- Flexible working arrangements and a global onboarding programme
- Learning platforms and multi-directional career paths
- Employee resource groups and a welcoming office in the Zurich-West district

Recruitment process

Apply online with your CV. After a conversation with our recruiter you meet the team, and then we make our decision.
