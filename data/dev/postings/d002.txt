Machine Learning Intern 2027 (Zurich)
Pinterest
Zurich

About Pinterest:

Pinterest is a visual discovery platform where people find ideas and plan what to do next. As a machine learning intern, you will use ML and AI to solve real product and engineering problems together with engineers, product managers and data scientists, and help prototype and improve ML-powered features.

What you'll do:
- Own a well-scoped ML project from defining the problem to running experiments, implementing the solution and presenting the results, with support from your mentor.
- Contribute to applied ML and AI research that shapes the Pinterest product.
- Collect and analyse data to build data-driven models.
- Write clean, maintainable code with documentation and tests.
- Apply techniques such as NLP or graph-based methods to modelling and ranking problems in discovery, ads and search.
- Break technical problems down, propose an approach and implement it.
- Deliver milestones, share your progress and ask for help when you need it.
- Use AI tools to speed up analysis and iteration, while checking their output carefully.

What we are looking for:
- You are working towards a Master's or PhD degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, NLP, Statistics, Information Sciences or a related field.
- Experience with machine learning in areas such as ranking, computer vision, NLP, recommendations, embeddings or information retrieval.
- Experience with big data technologies (e.g. Hadoop or Spark) and scalable real-time stream processing.
- Proficiency in at least one of the following: Java, C++, Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch or MLFlow.
- Experience with research and with solving analytical problems.
- You communicate well, work well in a team and find solutions to open-ended problems.

In-Office Requirement:

This internship is onsite in our Zurich office. The programme runs full-time during normal business hours from Monday to Friday, and we expect you in the office for in-person collaboration two to three weekdays per week. You need to live within commuting distance of Zurich.
