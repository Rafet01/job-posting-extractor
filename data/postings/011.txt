Job Description
The AI Research & Solutions department at Swissquote is looking for an MLOps Intern to help bring AI applications from experimentation to reliable, scalable production.

You will gain hands-on experience across the AI lifecycle, with a strong focus on the infrastructure and tooling behind LLM applications and AI agents, alongside traditional machine learning models.

During your internship, you will:

Improve monitoring and evaluation: Help automate the evaluation and monitoring of deployed AI applications. Develop observability for LLM responses, agent workflows and tool interactions, tracking quality, reliability, latency and cost.
Build deployment tooling: Contribute to our internal MLflow-based platform, helping engineers version, package and deploy models and agents on our infrastructure.
Automate pipelines: Build and improve reproducible data, training and evaluation pipelines using Argo Workflows, and contribute to automated testing and deployment.
Support AI governance: Help develop internal tools for tracking deployed AI applications, improving traceability and supporting company governance and compliance requirements.
Qualifications
Ongoing studies in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering or a related field.
Good Python skills and an interest in writing maintainable software.
Familiarity with—or a strong motivation to learn—CI/CD, GitHub Actions, Docker and Kubernetes.
Curiosity about LLMs and AI agents, particularly how they are deployed, evaluated and monitored in production.
An organized, proactive approach, a willingness to learn and the ability to collaborate effectively in English.
Experience with frontend development is a plus, as you may also contribute to interfaces for internal tools.
