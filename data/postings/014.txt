You love data, you are curious about AI, and you want to see your analysis go live? This internship puts you at the heart of customer data for the airlines of the Lufthansa Group. You will turn real customer behaviour into audiences for marketing campaigns and help us build AI agents that make our data work faster.

Starting date: January 01, 2027

Please apply in English.

Tasks

As an intern, you will work closely with data engineers, audience engineers, data analysts and marketing teams. Your job is to turn transactional, web, and app behaviour into customer insights and audiences that marketing teams use in their campaigns.

Analyse transactional, web, and app behaviour data in a customer data platform, using SQL and Python
Turn web behaviour into customer attributes and audiences for campaigns across Lufthansa, SWISS, Austrian Airlines and Brussels Airlines, and validate each audience against the data before it goes live
Measure how audiences perform in campaigns and present your findings to the team
Build and test AI agents that answer data questions and check data quality
Use AI coding assistants for SQL and Python, and check their output against the data
Work with consent-based customer data and learn how privacy rules shape every audience
Run your own data project with real customer data and support from the team
