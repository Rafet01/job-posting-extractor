About the job
Job Description

Location: Basel, Zurich, Geneva

Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100%

 Help our clients navigate the future with confidence and embrace decision making with clear data-driven choices 

Deloitte AI & Data helps clients unlock the power of AI and data to deliver better human-centric solutions that catalyse growth and efficiencies. We enable them to leverage data, analytics, modern platforms, and data science & AI technologies to uncover hidden relationships from vast troves of data, generate insights, and inform their decision-making. 

We help clients transform their business by designing and implementing data solutions to excel in their markets. Our role is to understand their business problems and use data-driven thinking and technology to achieve outcomes that drive their business forward.

How You Can Make An Impact

 Lead AI and analytics engagements from discovery to production 
 Design complex data & analytics transformations by providing architectural, technical and organizational thought leadership and guide clients in executing these transformations, which have significant organizational implications. 
 Serve as the business–IT translator, facilitating workshops, consolidating diverse requirements into coherent solution designs, and aligning stakeholders around clear decisions. 
 Manage cross-functional delivery teams (onshore/nearshore/offshore), ensuring delivery governance, quality assurance, risk management and effective communications. 
 Drive Generative and Agentic AI initiatives (where relevant), including LLM-enabled solutions, prompt engineering, RAG patterns, safety guardrails and evaluation approaches aligned to client policies. 
 Develop and maintain trusted client relationships through project delivery and account growth; support business development by shaping proposals, POVs and demos, and identifying new opportunities. 
 Be a role model for people you work with by demonstrating strong work ethic, leadership, and subject matter competence. 
 Grow expertise in different industry and subject matter areas and share your knowledge with the rest of Deloitte talent. 


 What We Are Looking For: 

 Drive and Motivation:  

 Passionate about building a career in consulting at the intersection of AI, analytics and business impact. 
 Ownership mindset with the ability to manage multiple activities in a fast-paced environment and deliver independently and through teams. 


 Consulting and Communication Skills:  

 Strong Microsoft Power Point and storytelling skills: Ability to design and present meaningful, executive-ready materials. 
 Strong Conceptual Capability: Ability to simplify complex problems and translate them into actionable solution designs and roadmaps. 
 Business-IT Translator: Proficient in requirements leadership, stakeholder alignment and facilitation of client workshops. 
 Team Collaboration and Leadership: Proven ability to guide nearshore and offshore technical implementation teams, ensuring effective collaboration and project success. 
 Technical Integration: Skilful in integrating system components, providing clear business explanations, and leading technical workshops. 


 Technical Expertise: 

 Strong Engineering and/or Mathematics background. 
 Solid experience with Python. Experience with R is a plus. 
 Experience delivering Generative AI, AI, Machine Learning and/or Analytics solutions on one or more of the cloud services providers: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Azure or Google Cloud Platform (GCP). 
 Familiarity with Large Language Models (LLMs) and adjacent services required to bring use cases to production on one or more of the cloud service providers above is highly preferred. 
 Experience with data science workbench tools such as Snowflake, Databricks and Dataiku is a plus. 
 Experience with Data Visualization tools such as PowerBI and Tableau is a plus. 


 Language Proficiency: 

 Fluent in English; fluency in German and/or French is highly preferred to facilitate communication with diverse teams and stakeholders. 


 Industry Experience: 

 At least 6 years of practical experience in consulting, tech companies, or industry-specific roles, particularly in data-driven fields. 
 Previous experience in the Banking, Insurance, Life Science or Healthcare sector is advantageous.
