About Abionic
Abionic is a Swiss diagnostics company developing rapid point-of-care tests for the early
detection of life-threatening conditions such as sepsis, leveraging its proprietary nanofluidic
technology.
Your Role
You will support the management and analysis of clinical study data.
• Extract and structure clinical study data
• Clean and validate datasets to ensure quality and consistency
• Prepare datasets for statistical analysis
• Contribute to exploratory analysis and the presentation of preliminary findings
Your Profile
• Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Biology, Life Sciences,
Biomedical Sciences, or a related field.
• Strong interest in clinical research, medical data, and data analysis.
• Analytical mindset, attention to detail, and ability to work independently
• Proficiency in Excel.
• Good command of English and French
Previous experience in clinical research or data management is appreciated but not required.
What We Offer
• Hands-on experience in an innovative Swiss MedTech company.
• Practical training in clinical data management, data quality assessment, and medical
data interpretation.
• Exposure to real-world clinical datasets and research methodologies.
• The opportunity to contribute to ongoing research activities involving Pancreatic
Stone Protein (PSP), a biomarker studied in the context of sepsis.
